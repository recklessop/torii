What these are for

A service identity is for something that isn't you — a cron job, a script, another product. It gets its own name in the audit log, so a year from now you can tell machine traffic from your own, and its own key you can rotate without touching your sign-in.

It can never reach anything you can't, and it stops working if your account is disabled. If you need something that outlives your account — production, say — an admin can promote it to an independent service.

If it's really just you running something by hand — Claude Code, a bit of curl — an API key is simpler and the audit correctly says it was you. Reach for a service identity when you want the log to name the thing, or when you'll want to revoke it without touching your own access.
