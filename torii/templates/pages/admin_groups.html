Create a group

A group is a named set of people and services. Grant a server to the group and every member gets it — grants stay per-tool, and a group can only ever widen what someone reaches, never narrow it. A per-connector or per-key limit still applies on top.

Leave the claim empty unless you already know the group name in your identity provider. It does nothing yet — no federated login is wired up — but a group with no claim can never be satisfied by one, which is the safe default.
